This page is deliberately long enough to exercise root scrolling and document-space hit testing.

Rendering pipeline

HTML becomes a DOM, CSS becomes computed style, boxes become layout geometry, inline content becomes fragments, and paint becomes a backend-neutral display list.

The interaction layer consumes final geometry instead of teaching the renderer about links or form controls.

Scroll section 1

Each wheel event updates a clamped root scroll offset. Layout remains cached while display-list coordinates are translated and clipped to the viewport.

Scroll section 2

Hit testing converts viewport coordinates back into document coordinates before walking the stable hit-test list in reverse paint order.

Scroll section 3

Pointer down records the pressed target and focus. Pointer up activates only when it lands on the same target.

Scroll section 4

Tab and Shift+Tab cycle a deterministic focus chain containing links, text inputs, checkboxes, and buttons.

Scroll section 5

GET form submission serializes live text and checkbox state, then starts an ordinary top-level navigation.

Scroll section 6

Back, forward, and reload re-fetch documents atomically. A failed load leaves the committed page and history cursor untouched.

Scroll section 7

The CPU and GPU renderers still consume the same display-list contract. Interaction remains backend-neutral.

Scroll section 8

The alpha is intentionally small, deterministic, and honest about unsupported web-platform features.

Scroll section 9

Static documentation and blogs are the target corpus for this release.
