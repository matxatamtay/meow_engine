Keyboard tour

Press Tab and Shift+Tab to move focus. Enter activates links and buttons. Space toggles a checkbox. Alt+Left and Alt+Right traverse history. Ctrl+R reloads.

Scope

This corpus remains script-free so interaction tests stay easy to inspect. The separate script-alpha corpus exercises Boa and DOM bindings.
